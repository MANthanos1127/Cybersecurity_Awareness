Stay One Step Ahead of Cybercriminals

Learn about common cyber threats, discover practical safety tips, and know where to seek help if you become a victim.

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, mobile phones, networks, software, and personal data from cyber attacks, hackers, viruses, and online fraud.

It helps individuals and organizations keep their information safe, prevent unauthorized access, and reduce the risk of financial loss and identity theft. Good cybersecurity habits make the internet a safer place for everyone.

Top 10 Cyber Threats

  1. Phishing Attacks
    Fake emails, messages or websites steal personal information.
  2. Lottery & Prize Scams
    Scammers promise fake prizes to trick people into sending money.
  3. Online Shopping Fraud
    Fake shopping websites collect payment but never deliver products.
  4. Remote Access App Scams
    Criminals ask victims to install apps and take control of their devices.
  5. Credit/Debit Card Fraud
    Stolen card details are used for unauthorized transactions.
  6. UPI & Digital Payment Fraud
    Fake QR codes and payment requests steal money.
  7. Sextortion
    Criminals blackmail victims using private photos or videos.
  8. Identity Theft
    Personal information is stolen and misused for fraud.
  9. Fake Banking Calls
    Fraudsters pretend to be bank officials and ask for OTPs or passwords.
  10. Investment & Money-Doubling Scams
    Fake investment schemes promise huge profits to cheat people.

Stay Safe Online

  1. Never share OTP, PIN, Password or CVV.
  2. Verify website URLs.
  3. Use strong passwords.
  4. Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
  5. Avoid suspicious links.
  6. Download apps from trusted sources.
  7. Keep software updated.
  8. Beware of fake offers.
  9. Check bank transactions regularly.
  10. Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking.
